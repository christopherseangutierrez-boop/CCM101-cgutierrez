### Mission Analysis

In this lab, I found Google Cloud Platform (GCP) the most impressive because it’s built with engineers in mind and leads the way in containerization and AI. Its Kubernetes service (GKE) and Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) make it really strong for modern cloud setups. Even though AWS, Azure, and GCP compete with each other, they all share the same basics: virtual machines for computing, scalable storage, private networks, and identity management. They also use pay‑as‑you‑go pricing and multi‑region backups to keep systems running smoothly.

Working like a Cloud Solutions Consultant showed me that choosing a cloud provider isn’t about picking the most popular one—it’s about matching the platform to the actual needs of the business, like budget, existing systems, compliance rules, and growth plans. For example, a university that already uses Microsoft tools would benefit from Azure, while an AI research lab would get more out of GCP’s advanced hardware.

From this activity, I learned how to compare cloud platforms fairly, spot equivalent services across providers, and design cloud architectures based on business goals. It also helped me improve my GitHub portfolio by adding organized multi‑cloud reports, client advisory notes, and clear documentation that make my work look more professional and ready for real projects.
