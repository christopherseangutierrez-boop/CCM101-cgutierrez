### Mission Analysis

Among the three major platforms, **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** impressed me the most due to its engineering-focused design and industry leadership in containerization and AI. Its native Kubernetes platform (GKE) and custom Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) provide exceptional performance for modern cloud-native architectures.

Despite their market positioning, **AWS, Azure, and GCP** share fundamental similarities[cite: 1]. All three providers build their foundation on core infrastructure primitives: virtualized compute instances, scalable object storage, private software-defined networks, and role-based identity management[cite: 1]. Furthermore, each vendor employs a pay-as-you-go pricing model with multi-region redundancy to ensure business continuity[cite: 1].

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant demonstrated that **business requirements must strictly dictate cloud platform selection**[cite: 1]. Technical decision-making is not about picking the most popular cloud provider, but choosing the platform that aligns with financial budgets, operational legacy codebases, regulatory compliance, and growth targets[cite: 1]. For instance, a Microsoft-centric university benefits significantly from Azure[cite: 1], while an AI research laboratory requires GCP's specialized computing acceleration[cite: 1].

Through this laboratory, I learned how to evaluate cloud platforms objectively, map equivalent services across vendor lock-in boundaries, and translate business needs into technical cloud architecture blueprints[cite: 1]. Finally, completing this mission has elevated my **GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio** by adding structured multi-cloud comparative reports, client advisory frameworks, and clean documentation[cite: 1].
